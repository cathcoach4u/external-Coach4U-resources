📅 Annual Review

Set aside an hour together. Reflect honestly on your year as a couple before looking ahead.

🏆 What did we achieve together?
Wins, milestones, goals reached, moments you’re proud of…
🤔 What didn’t go as planned?
Honest reflection — not blame. What fell short or stayed stuck?
💡 What did we learn?
About each other, about yourselves, about how you work as a team…
⬆️ What do we want more of next year?
Experiences, feelings, habits, time together…
⬇️ What do we want less of?
Drains, patterns, habits, or dynamics to let go of…
🌟 Our word for next year
One word or short phrase that captures the feeling or intention you want to carry into next year.
